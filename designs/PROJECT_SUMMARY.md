# 🌞 Helios Ten-Chi - Project Summary

## 💡 Design Decisions Explained

### Dark Theme
**Why:** Professional, modern, reduces eye strain, makes colors pop

### Solar Animations
**Why:** Reinforces energy theme, adds dynamism, engaging without distracting

### Gradients
**Why:** Modern aesthetic, energy flow metaphor, brand differentiation

### Large Typography
**Why:** Readability, hierarchy, confidence, modern web standards

### Card-Based Layout
**Why:** Scannable, flexible, mobile-friendly, clear content grouping

### Minimal Icons
**Why:** Clean aesthetic, universal understanding, fast loading
